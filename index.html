How HouseTab could make money

This is the plan. Nothing here is live and there are no users yet.

Free: every house of up to 5 people

Add bills, split them, see who owes what, join by QR code. Free keeps the whole house on the same list. A list that only some people use does not work.

House Plus (planned)

€1.99 per house, per month

Polite automatic reminders, so nobody has to chase. Bank-transfer links. A monthly summary to show a landlord.

Who pays

The housemate who is tired of chasing. One person paying €1.99 for the house is easier than five people paying €0.40 each.

Why you can trust it

  • It holds no money. It only keeps a list.
  • Everyone in the house sees the same list.
  • Every bill and payment shows who added it. If someone deletes one, it stays on the list, crossed out, with their name.
